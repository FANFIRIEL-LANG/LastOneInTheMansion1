# Narrator audio pack — Russian

Status: script prepared; recordings are not yet generated.

## Voice direction

One Russian-speaking male narrator, approximately 35–50 years old. Low-mid warm baritone, slightly textured but clear, intimate and human rather than theatrical. Calm confidence, restrained menace, subtle dry humor. Natural Russian stress and connected speech; no synthetic announcer cadence, no exaggerated horror whisper, no overacting.

Performance:
- Speak as a host who is physically in the mansion with the players.
- Vary phrasing and sentence rhythm. Let short sentences land.
- Use real pauses between story beats, not a pause after every word.
- Keep consonants clear on an iPhone speaker; avoid excessive bass and reverb.
- Use restrained emotion: curiosity in discoveries, tension before night, warmth when welcoming players.
- Do not imitate a known actor, celebrity, or copyrighted character.

## Production settings

- Language: Russian (ru-RU).
- Generate each clip as a separate file; do not concatenate the entire game into one recording.
- Preferred format: MP3, 44.1 kHz, 128–192 kbps, mono or stereo.
- Normalize loudness consistently; leave headroom and avoid clipping.
- Keep narration dry/close. Add quiet ambience as a separate optional loop, never baked into speech.
- All clips must be bundled in the app so the game works fully offline.
- Never narrate player names, secret roles, private clues, cult selections, investigation results, or private night actions.
- Dynamic names and counts stay as on-screen text; do not generate them through TTS at runtime.

## Technical playback requirements

1. Use local files under `public/audio/narrator/`; never request speech from a cloud API during a game.
2. A clip must be played only after a user interaction on iOS, to respect WebKit audio activation rules.
3. Add pause/resume and a visible mute toggle. Changing screens must stop the previous clip.
4. If a clip is missing or playback fails, show the narration text and continue the game; do not block a turn.
5. Audio must never contain private role names, private evidence, investigation results, selected targets, or any other hidden game state.
6. Keep sound effects and ambience on separate volume controls from narration.
